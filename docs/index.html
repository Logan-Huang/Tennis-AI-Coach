Privacy Policy

TennisAI: Swing Coach · Last updated July 29, 2026

The short version

TennisAI: Swing Coach does not collect your data. Everything the app records and analyzes stays on your device. There is no account, no login, no server, and no analytics or advertising code of any kind.

What the app does with your video

When you record or import a video, the app analyzes it entirely on your iPhone or iPad using Apple's on-device Vision framework to detect your body position and identify tennis strokes. The video never leaves your device as part of this analysis.

Videos, the analysis results derived from them, and your session history are stored in the app's private storage area on your device. You can delete any session from within the app, and deleting the app removes all of it.

Permissions the app asks for, and why

PermissionWhy it's needed
Camera To record your strokes for analysis.
Microphone To capture audio alongside your video so playback sounds right.
Photo library
(add only)		 To save an annotated video or report card to your photo library, when you choose to. This is add-only access — the app cannot read or browse your existing photos.

You can revoke any of these at any time in Settings, though the app's recording features will not work without camera access.

When you choose to share

The app can export a report card, an annotated video, or session data. If you tap Share, iOS shows the standard system share sheet and you choose the destination — Messages, Mail, another app, or saving to Files or Photos. The data goes only where you send it. The developer does not receive a copy and has no visibility into what you share or with whom.

If you save to your photo library and have iCloud Photos enabled, Apple may sync that item to your own iCloud account under Apple's privacy policy. That is your account, not ours, and we have no access to it.

Third-party code

There is none. The app uses only frameworks provided by Apple as part of iOS. No analytics SDKs, no advertising networks, no crash reporters, and no third-party vendors of any kind have been added to this app. Consequently, no third party receives data from this app.

Tracking

The app does not track you. It does not access the advertising identifier (IDFA), does not build a profile of you, and does not link any data to you or your device for advertising or measurement purposes across other companies' apps or websites.

Children

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

Your data rights

Because no data is transmitted off your device, there is nothing for us to retrieve, correct, export, or delete on your behalf. You have direct and complete control: delete individual sessions in the app, or delete the app to remove everything.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app adds a feature that transmits data off the device, this policy will be updated before that version ships, and the app's App Store privacy disclosures will be updated to match.
